# Nekoma's Fixed

[Nekoma's Fixed](https://modrinth.com/mod/nekomas-fixed) is a Fabric mod by GreenJAB, built on the YouTuber Nekoma's ideas, with textures by velocyblue. The pack runs its own NeoForge port of the blocks and items it adds; the changes it makes to vanilla aren't in. Everything is in its own creative tab, and JEI (**R** and **U**) shows every recipe.

## Four new colours

**Amber, aqua, indigo and maroon dyes.** Two torchflowers make four maroon, two pitcher plants four indigo; maroon and white make amber, indigo and white make aqua.

- They colour what vanilla's dyes colour, with the usual recipes: wool, carpets, terracotta and glazed terracotta, concrete and concrete powder, stained glass and panes, candles (and the cakes they go on), beds and shulker boxes.
- **Dyeing a shulker box keeps what's in it.** A cauldron washes one back to plain, and a dispenser places one, as with vanilla's.
- New-coloured wool, carpets and beds dye back to vanilla's colours.
- Signs, fireworks and sheep keep to vanilla's sixteen colours, and villagers don't sleep in the new beds.

## Dyed bricks

Bricks, slabs, stairs and walls in all 20 colours: eight bricks round a dye make eight, and their slabs, stairs and walls come from the crafting grid or the stonecutter. They're called "Dyed ... Bricks", since Create Deco already has its own Blue Bricks. Mine them with a pickaxe.

## Froglights

Seventeen new froglights, Clear to Sakura. **Any froglight and a dye make another**, vanilla's three included, so you need frogs only for your first.

## Glow Torch

A glow ink sac on a stick makes four. It burns under water, nearly as bright as a torch on land, and gives no light out of it: the light for underwater builds.

## Redstone Striker

Right-click a block with it to power that spot at full strength for 16 ticks, or a single tick if you sneak.

- Dust there reads 15, a block there powers what's next to it, and a lamp, door, piston or dispenser there turns on. A struck observer pulses.
- 64 uses. Two redstone dust, a gold ingot and a flint make one.
- A lone dot or cross of dust changes shape when you right-click it (vanilla does that too), so strike dust that's part of a line.
- It lights no fires: it's for redstone, not a flint and steel.

## Clocks you can hang up

**Right-click a block with a vanilla clock** to set it on the floor or a wall. Safe zones stop it as they stop any block.

- Right-click a placed clock for the time, as HH:MM. A comparator next to it reads the hour.
- Put a bell on a floor clock to make it an alarm; shears take the bell off, and breaking the clock drops it.
- **Right-click the air with a clock to set an alarm time:** it records the time of day and glints. Placed, it gives a redstone pulse at that time every day. Right-click the air again to forget it.

## The kiln

A furnace for building blocks, twice as fast: glazed terracotta, stone and smooth stone, glass, bricks, cracked bricks, smooth sandstone and quartz, and more. Each pays the furnace's own XP, and it gives off light while it burns. It has no recipe book, so look its recipes up in JEI. Mud bricks round a furnace, on terracotta, make one.
