# Building and decorating

Every block you place by hand trains **Construction**, and the fancier the block, the more it pays ([Skills](skills.md#construction)). This page is about the building itself: the blocks the pack adds, and the tools that save you the clicks. JEI (**R** and **U** over an item) shows every recipe, and Create's blocks have a Ponder scene (hold **W** over one).

## Before you start

- **Nothing is claimed.** PvP is on and anyone can break what you build; anyone with a wrench can pocket your Create machines in a moment, too. Build where you can keep an eye on things.
- **Safe zones.** Nobody can build or break inside [Lemurton](lemurton.md#a-safe-zone) or [Kiln Hollow](fight-pits.md#kiln-hollow).
- **Place by hand for XP.** Construction XP comes from the blocks you place yourself (the Builder's Wand counts). Deployers and schematicannons earn nobody XP.
- **If you're eliminated.** Accepting elimination removes every block you ever placed; blocks a schematicannon placed stay. See [Hearts, graves and elimination](lifesteal.md#elimination).
- **Skill levels.** Some tools here wait on a skill level: a schematicannon takes Construction 30 to make, and using the Builder's Wand takes Crafting 30. An item's tooltip says in red what it needs.

## Copycats

A copycat is a block that wears another block's look. Make them from zinc ingots in a stonecutter (JEI shows every shape). Place one, then right-click it with a full block to give it that look; right-click again with the same block to turn it or change its shape. A wrench takes the material back out.

- **Create's own:** the **Copycat Panel** (it also takes bars and trapdoors) and the **Copycat Step**.
- **Copycats+** adds dozens more: slabs, stairs, slopes, layers, slices, bytes, panels, boards, beams, boxes, catwalks, fences, walls, panes and ladders, many in vertical versions too. Most turn into each other in a crafting grid.
- **Several blocks in one.** Double slabs, bytes, byte panels, half panels, half layers and boards take a different block in each part. Hold **Left Alt** as you right-click to fill every part at once. (Left Alt also opens the Relics ability wheel and the toolbox menu; rebind one in Controls if they get in the way.)
- **Grow them.** Right-click a layer, half layer or slice with another of the same to make it bigger, up to a full block.
- **Copycats that work.** Copycat doors (plain, iron, sliding and folding), trapdoors, fence gates, ladders, fluid pipes, shafts and cogwheels work like the real ones. Copycat buttons and pressure plates make switches nobody spots.
- **The Ghost Block** looks solid, but you walk straight through it: a hidden doorway.
- **Connected textures.** Copycats join their textures up with their neighbours. On ones whose tooltip says "CT Toggle", sneak + right-click with an empty hand switches that off.

## Placing faster

- **Placement assist.** Hold a shaft, cogwheel, girder, piston pole, metal ladder, sail, display board, table cloth, copycat panel or step, drill, saw, deployer or roller, and look at one already placed: a ghost shows where the next goes, and a right-click puts it there, even out over thin air (up to 12 blocks out). Create Deco's catwalks, railings and supports and many Copycats+ shapes do it too. More of Create's building tricks (casings by hand, encased shafts, belts) are in [Create tips](create-tips.md#building-faster).
- **Builder's Wand** ([Gear](gear.md#tools)). Right-click a face: every block of the same kind joined to it on that face gets a copy in front, up to 32 at once, taken from your inventory at 1 durability each (512 in all). It places plain blocks, so stairs, logs and anything else that faces a way come out facing the default way, and it does nothing on chests, machines, signs and the like.
- **Wand of Symmetry.** Keep it in your hotbar and every block you place within 50 blocks of its mirror is placed again on the other side. Right-click a surface to put the mirror there (or move it), sneak + right-click to choose **Mirror Once**, **Rectangular** or **Octagonal**, and right-click the air to remove it. The copies come out of your inventory.
- **Never run out.** Within 10 blocks of a **toolbox**, middle-click a block you don't carry and the toolbox hands you one. A backpack you're wearing with an **Advanced Refill Upgrade** in it does the same.
- **Reach further.** A **Potion of Reaching** (brew a crab claw into an awkward potion; Friends & Foes crabs live on coasts and in wetlands) lets you reach blocks further away.
- **A flying scaffold.** A harnessed [happy ghast](travel.md#the-happy-ghast) holds still while you stand on its back.

## Schematics

Save a build, then have a machine copy it, block for block.

1. **Schematic and Quill.** Right-click two corners of the build (hold **Ctrl** and scroll to pick a point in mid-air). **Ctrl + scroll** on a face of the box to grow or shrink it, then right-click again to name and save it. The file goes in the `schematics` folder of your game folder.
2. **Schematic Table.** Put an empty schematic in and pick your file to write it on. The server takes files up to 256 KB and keeps your last 10.
3. **Position it.** Hold the schematic and its tools show on screen: hold **Left Alt** and scroll to pick one. Right-click the ground to place it (hold **Ctrl** for a fixed distance), and **Ctrl + scroll** while pointing at it to move it, raise it, turn it or mirror it. Sneak + right-click to type exact coordinates.
4. **Schematicannon.** Put the schematic in, and gunpowder in its fuel slot: one gunpowder fires 400 blocks. It takes blocks from the chests beside it and says which item it's missing. Its settings choose what it may replace, whether to skip missing blocks, and whether to leave chests and the like alone.

**A shopping list.** Put a book or a clipboard in the cannon's Checklist Printer slot for a list of everything the build needs. Take that clipboard to a Stock Keeper and it can order everything on the list that your network has in stock.

## Blocks to build with

The block mods in the pack, and what's special about each.

### Create's own

- **Stone.** Andesite, granite, diorite, calcite, dripstone, deepslate, tuff, limestone, scoria, scorchia, crimsite, ochrum, veridium and asurine each come cut, polished cut, as cut bricks and small bricks, layered and as a pillar, with slabs, stairs and walls. A stonecutter makes them, or a mechanical saw facing up.
- **Glass and windows.** Framed glass (plain, horizontal and vertical), tiled glass, and windows in every wood and in ornate, industrial and weathered iron.
- **Metal.** Andesite, brass and copper bars, ladders, scaffolding and doors; metal girders (a shaft can run through one); industrial and weathered iron blocks; copper shingles and tiles, which weather like copper.
- **Connected textures.** Casings, framed glass, windows, shingles, tiles, scaffolding, vaults and fluid tanks join into one pattern when you place them side by side.
- **Colours.** Seats, sails, belts, valve handles and nixie tubes take dye: right-click them with it. On a display board, each line can take its own colour.
- **Placard.** A brass frame that shows an item: right-click it with the item, punch it to take the item out. Right-clicking it with the same item again gives a short redstone pulse.

### Create Deco

- **Metal sets** in andesite, brass, copper, iron, industrial iron and zinc: catwalks with railings and stairs, supports and wedges, train hull plating, sheet metal, mesh fences, bars, windows and panes, doors, trapdoors and ladders. Catwalks, railings and supports use placement assist.
- **Bricks** in eight colours (red, scarlet, blue, umber, dean, dusk, verdant and pearl): short, long, tiled and corner bricks, cracked and mossy ones, with stairs, slabs and walls.
- **Cage lamps.** The light that goes in sets the colour: a torch gives yellow, a soul torch blue, a redstone torch red, glow berries green. A placed lamp stays dark until the block it hangs on gets a redstone signal. Right-click it with an empty hand to flip that: then it's lit until powered.
- **Doors.** Create Deco's metal doors open by hand. Craft one with a redstone torch for a **Locked** door that only opens with redstone.
- **Decals.** Warning signs and arrows, cut from iron sheets in a stonecutter.
- **Shipping containers.** Item vaults in 16 colours.
- **Coins aren't money.** Create Deco's Gold, Iron, Brass, Copper, Zinc, Industrial Iron and Netherite Coins and Coin Stacks are decoration only. The server's money is the Gold Coins vendors pay ([Coins, vendors and trading](economy.md)).

### Macaw's mods

Macaw's adds roofs, windows, doors, trapdoors, fences and walls, bridges, stairs and balconies, paths and pavings, lights and furniture, in the vanilla woods and stones. Create Deco and Macaw's blocks pay 15 Construction XP each.

| Blocks | Good to know |
|---|---|
| Chairs, stools, couches | Right-click to sit. Couches placed side by side join up |
| Cabinets, drawers, storage counters | Hold items: right-click to open |
| Lights | Right-click a lamp to switch it off and on. Paper lamps and other dyeable lights recolour with a right-click of dye; tiki torches and the lights whose tooltip says so stack up taller |
| Fences and walls | Stack upward to make taller ones. Wired fences hurt anything that walks into them |
| Doors | Metal doors need redstone to open. A garage door goes on the ceiling and rolls down when opened |
| Windows | **One Way Glass**: place it from the inside, looking out, and you see out while from outside it looks solid. Arrow slits to shoot through. Curtains, blinds and shutters open and close with a right-click |
| Bridges | Pieces join into sides and corners as you place them; bridge lanterns and torches go on the handrails |
| Stairs | Railings go on the side of any stairs; up to 4 balconies fit on one block; platforms make the corners |

### Woodworks and Every Compat

- **Woodworks** gives every wood its own bookshelves, chiseled bookshelves, ladders, beehives, boards, leaf piles and chests, and adds the **Sawmill** (see above).
- **Every Compat** copies Macaw's furniture, Woodworks' blocks, Farmer's Delight cabinets and Create's windows into every modded wood, Regions Unexplored's maple, redwood and willow among them. Macaw's other blocks come in the vanilla woods only.

### Other block sets

| Mod | Blocks |
|---|---|
| Vanilla Backport | Pale oak (from the Pale Garden), resin bricks (smelt resin clumps), sulfur and cinnabar stone, leaf litter (smelt any leaves), wildflowers, firefly bushes, cactus flowers, dry grass |
| Farmer's Delight | Canvas signs (dye one at a crafting table for a coloured background), canvas rugs, tatami mats, straw bales |
| Create Aeronautics | Envelopes, a balloon cloth: right-click them with dye to paint them, and a fan blowing through water washes them white again |

## Keeping it safe

Nothing stops a player who's set on breaking in, but these slow them down:

- **Light it up.** Hold or ring a **Haunted Bell** and the nearby spots dark enough for monsters to spawn light up. Make one by placing a Peculiar Bell right above soul fire.
- **Doors that need redstone.** Create Deco's Locked doors and Macaw's metal doors only open with a redstone signal: hide the switch with a copycat button.
- **Hidden ways in.** A Copycat Ghost Block looks like part of the wall and lets you walk through.
- **Windows.** One Way Glass lets you see out while nobody sees in, and arrow slits let you shoot out.
- **Fences.** Wired fences hurt anything that walks into them.

## Renewable blocks

- **Limestone and scoria.** Where lava meets Create's honey it sets into limestone; where it meets chocolate, scoria. Build them like a cobblestone generator.
- **Coloured concrete.** Mix a dye with water in a basin for liquid dye (Create: Dragons Plus). Where liquid dye meets lava, poured or from a Create pipe, it sets into concrete of that colour: a concrete generator.
- **Trees in tight spots.** **Tree Fertilizer** on a sapling grows the tree even where it wouldn't have room.

## The Construction skill

[Skills](skills.md#construction) has the details: what each block pays, the bulk recipes and the saving perk. In short:

- A block you place pays XP once it has stood for a minute, once per spot: pulling it down and putting it back pays nothing. Fancy blocks pay the most.
- Construction unlocks bulk recipes at the crafting table: ten stone bricks from eight stone and a clay ball, for one.
- At 99 the Construction Cape gives +1 block reach and comes with the **Mason's Palette**: right-click the air to pick a block (its picker has a search box), then place as many as you like. Middle-click a block to pick that kind.
