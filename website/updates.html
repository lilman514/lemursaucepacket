Updates

What's new

The pack is on version 1.16.1. The launcher installs every update before you play, so there's nothing to download by hand: this page is just the story so far.

  1. 1.16.12026-10-08

    The hiscores are live on this website: everyone on the server ranked by every skill, total and combat level, quest points, bosses and collections, from what the server sends every half hour while anyone plays (in game, /hiscores is always up to the minute).

  2. 1.16.02026-10-08

    A fresh world, and new worlds start you in easy land: a forest or plains whose trees anyone can chop, with no acacia, jungle, desert, snow or mountains close by. Create's machines pay skill XP to everyone within 16 blocks of them, as the same work pays by hand (a cobblestone generator a tenth of it), and the new XP Bank keeps 10, 25 or 50% of it, by tier, while nobody's near. Nekoma's Fixed joins the pack: four new dye colours for every dyed block, dyed bricks, seventeen froglights, a torch that burns under water, the Redstone Striker, clocks you can hang up and a kiln.

  3. 1.15.02026-10-08

    Every modded item has a place in the skill progression: 147 gates over 496 items. Create's machines, the Crafter and placed backpacks work at the levels of whoever last placed or right-clicked them, and backpack upgrades, relic abilities and the Totem of Undying wait for their level, with a chat line and a ding saying what's missing. The wiki gained a new FAQ, tips pages and 111 biome photos; backpacks start closed to other players, and the Gold Coins have a lemur on them.

  4. 1.14.32026-10-08

    Photos in the wiki's atlas, taken in game: 207 of the 227 biomes and all 119 creatures. The server builds the land past your render distance with a lot less work.

  5. 1.14.02026-10-07

    A skills screen like RuneScape's (ESC → Skills, or /skills), with every skill's guide; the wiki in a browser inside the game; Distant Horizons draws the land 2,048 blocks out; auto walk on the ` key; the HUD editor resizes as well as moves; and a new relic, the Climbing Boots, steps you up full blocks.

  6. 1.13.02026-10-07

    The hiscores: everyone on the server ranked RuneScape-style, by every skill, total and combat level, quest points, capes, the collection log, monsters slain, bosses (Elvarg, Kiln-Tok-Jad, Kiln-Kal-Zuk, the Ender Dragon, the Wither, the Warden and more) and collections (music discs, armour trims, pottery sherds, relics, mob heads). /hiscores shows them in game, and /hiscores player <name> anyone's. Create's machines now wait on the skill they take over: Mining 15 for mechanical drills, Woodcutting 15 for saws, Farming 15 for harvesters and ploughs, Cooking 20 for encased fans, Crafting 30 for mechanical crafters, Construction 30 for the schematicannon, Smithing 35 for steam engines, Agility 35 for trains, Enchanting 35 for the blaze enchanter and 50 for the printer, and Mining 50 and up for the ore vein drill heads. Anyone can use a machine someone else made. Construction's saving perk now only ever saves building blocks: never storage blocks, copper, coin stacks or anything that holds items.

  7. 1.12.02026-10-07

    Construction is the sixteenth skill: every block you place pays XP once it has stood for a minute, and the fancier the block the more (cobblestone pays 2, Macaw's roofs and furniture 15, a lectern or a sea lantern 25); placing and breaking pays nothing. Each level adds a 0.1% chance a block isn't used up, and Construction unlocks twelve crafting recipes for decorative blocks in bulk (ten stone bricks from eight stone and a clay ball, cracked and mossy kinds without a furnace, eight stairs where vanilla gives four). At 99 the Construction Cape comes with the Mason's Palette: choose a block (stone of every kind, bricks, quartz, concrete, glass, Create's cut stone, Create Deco, Macaw's stone and metal blocks, lanterns, lecterns) and place as many as you like. What it places drops nothing when broken, so it can't be farmed. Lost it? Gerta the Mason makes another. The Maxed Cape now asks for Construction 99 too. The Builder's Wand and the Harvester's Scythe now respect safe zones like Lemurton.

  8. 1.11.02026-10-07

    Every skill now unlocks things on the way to 99, like a RuneScape skill guide: Crafting 30 makes diamond gear, Smithing 50 netherite, the bigger meals wait on Cooking, and Farming and Woodcutting gate the rarer crops and trees. Brewing is the fifteenth skill: each potion you take out of a stand trains it, each ingredient waits on a level, and Brewing 55 makes Eyes of Ender, so it's also the way to the Ender Dragon. Wither skeletons drop skulls only at combat level 75, treasure on the line needs Fishing 20, and crossbows and tridents moved to Ranged. Enchantments past their vanilla level also need the gear's own skill to use: Fishing for Lure, Mining for Fortune, Attack for Sharpness. Your combat level shows under your name. The bigger main quests ask for levels first (Dragon Slayer I: combat 40 and Brewing 55). Each gear set takes a hand-made part, and machines (the Crafter, Create's crafters, mixer and press) won't make anything gated. The Brewing Cape makes potions last half as long again.

  9. 1.10.02026-10-07

    Capes are items now. Earn one and it goes into your bag: right-click it, or put it in the new Cape slot in the Curios panel, and only someone who earned it can wear it. Hang any cape on an armor stand. ESC → Capes is your collection: every cape, how far along you are, each perk, and who makes another if you lose one (Odo the Tailor in Lemurton, Brakka at Kiln Hollow for the pit capes). Every Fight Pits or Inferno win hands over another cape. The Infernal Cape burns from the hem up, so it no longer looks like the Fire Cape, and the Gold Coins are drawn in vanilla's own gold. Lemurton's townsfolk now update themselves when their dialogs change.

  10. 1.9.02026-10-07

    Two dragon quests. Dragon Slayer I is now the Ender Dragon: the three pieces make a Map to the Stronghold, and everyone with the map who is near her when she falls gets her head for Oziach. Dragon Slayer II follows it: Wizard Traiborn scries Crandor with her bottled breath, and Elvarg's Lair is its fight. Dragon armour now waits on Dragon Slayer II, the Fight Pits on Dragon Slayer I, and Ashes of the Kiln asks for Dragon Slayer II in place of the Ender Dragon. Lost the Anti-dragon Shield? The mayor has another made.

  11. 1.8.02026-10-07

    The Fight Pits and the Inferno. After Dragon Slayer I, the Kilnfolk test you at Kiln Hollow: thirty waves alone, then Kiln-Tok-Jad, whose fire you block with a shield and whose slam you run from, for the animated Fire Cape. Ashes of the Kiln (the Wither, the Ender Dragon, the Warden and an Infernal Key) opens the Inferno, where Kiln-Kal-Zuk burns everyone his drifting shield doesn't cover, for the Infernal Cape. Fall in either and you're pulled out with everything you had. The legendary capes are redrawn as pixel art, and every armour set looks like itself when worn.

  12. New world2026-10-07

    A fresh world for everyone. Lemurton and the Crandor memorial were built anew, and the old world is kept safe as a backup.

  13. 1.7.02026-10-06

    Elvarg's Lair: speak to Ned at the new Crandor memorial and fight Elvarg in a lair of your own, alone or with up to three friends. If she gets you, Ned keeps everything you had: nothing drops and nothing goes into a grave.

  14. 1.6.22026-10-06

    A new XP tracker: a card per skill with its icon, the XP counting up as you work, and a gold "Level N!" when you go up.

  15. 1.6.12026-10-06

    Elvarg rises at full strength, and the Anti-dragon Shield now puts out her fire as well as stopping most of her breath.

  16. 1.6.02026-10-06

    Main quests, RuneScape style: Cook's Assistant, The Knight's Sword and Dragon Slayer I, given by Lemurton's people. Ice and Fire's dragons arrive: fire, ice and lightning, sleeping on their hoards far from spawn.

  17. 1.5.12026-10-06

    Shop buttons show their whole tooltip.

  18. 1.5.02026-10-06

    The economy: Gold Coins in one stack, vendors who buy anything and sell their own goods, townsfolk who talk before they trade, and /trade between players.

  19. 1.4.12026-10-06

    Hand-built city walls, SkyBlock-style nametags over the townsfolk, and JEI hides items you can't get.

  20. 1.4.02026-10-06

    Lemurton moves a short walk from spawn (you get a compass to find it), and every town in the world has a name of its own.

  21. 1.3.02026-10-06

    Lemurton rebuilt from Luki's Grand Capitals: a proper walled city with a cathedral, a market square and gatehouses.

  22. 1.2.92026-10-05

    Lemurton's townsfolk move in: the mayor, market traders, shopkeepers, guards, and the first questline, Welcome to Lemurton.

  23. 1.2.82026-10-05

    The first Lemurton, and safe zones where nobody can build, break or fight.

  24. 1.2.42026-09-30

    A rare crash while the game starts is gone.

  25. 1.2.32026-09-30

    A preview in the HUD editor, and crafting fixes.

  26. 1.2.22026-09-30

    HUD Layout in the ESC menu: move or hide every part of the screen.

  27. 1.2.12026-09-30

    Every death drops a Heart, and bare fists break logs again.

  28. 1.2.02026-09-30

    Enchanting becomes a skill, Hardness tomes gate the harder ores, lifesteal hearts and graves arrive, waystones replace teleports, and the quest book is rebuilt.

  29. 1.1.02026-09-29

    The Armory opens: Create-made tools, five armour sets with set bonuses, earned capes, and the guide book in game and on the wiki.
