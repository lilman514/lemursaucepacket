# Quests

The quest book (ESC → Quests) is a road map, not a gate: nothing is locked behind it. Every quest pays **skill XP** and most pay **coins**; the big gear-shaped quests at the end of a chapter pay the most.

![The quest book](images/quest-book.png)

## Chapters

**The Ages** — the main line, one chapter per stage of progress:

| Chapter | What it teaches | Ends with |
|---|---|---|
| Landfall | Rules, first tools, first backpack, a village | A wrench |
| First Rotation | Water wheels, cogs, the millstone and press | Andesite alloy at scale |
| Brass Age | Blaze burners, mixing, brass and precision | The mechanical crafter |
| Banners | Raids, ominous banners, the illager threat | A pillager outpost cleared |
| Iron Roads | Trains, stations, signals, freight | A working line between bases |
| Skyward | Create Aeronautics: propellers, envelopes, levitite, steering | Maiden voyage |
| Crown of Fire | The Nether, netherite, the blaze economy | A netherite tool |
| Legacy | The End, the dragon, and what comes after | The dragon egg on a pedestal |

**Field Guides** — side chapters you dip into: Backpack Workshop, Homestead (farming and cooking), Bestiary (the new mobs), Atlas (exploring, structures, ore veins) and Coin & Commerce (Numismatics).

Each chapter opens with a card that says what the questline is for and what finishing it lets you do.

## How rewards work

- Coins arrive as Numismatics spurs/bevels/cogs; keep them in a bank card or a coin purse.
- Skill XP goes to the skill that fits the quest (mining quests pay Mining, and so on).
- Team progress is shared: if you're in a team, everyone gets the quest and its rewards.
