# Waystones

Waystones are standing stones you can warp between. Right-click one to activate it. From then on you can warp to it from any other waystone, or with a warp stone or a scroll.

## Where to find them

- **Villages.** Towns and Towers villages have one in the square. Some vanilla villages have one too.
- **Towers and shrines.** On top of the tall Towers of the Wild towers, on some of Structory's towers and outposts, and at Dungeons and Taverns shrines.
- **The wild.** Now and then one stands alone, a few hundred blocks from the next at the closest. Its look follows the biome: sandy in deserts, mossy in jungles and swamps, blackstone in the Nether and on volcanoes, end stone in the End.

Waystones you've activated show on your minimap and world map, with their names. So do global waystones and sharestones. Ones you haven't found yet don't.

## Taking one home

- **Found waystones can be taken.** Mine one with a pickaxe and it drops as an item.
- With **Silk Touch** it keeps its name, and everyone who activated it can still warp to it. Without, it becomes a fresh waystone of your own.
- **Restyle** any waystone on a crafting table: the waystone plus 3 of a style's stone (stone bricks, mossy stone bricks, chiseled sandstone, deepslate, blackstone, end stone bricks, red nether bricks, purpur, prismarine or mud bricks).
- There are no claims. Anyone can take your waystone too, so keep it where you can watch it.

## Building one

It's meant to be hard. Everything starts with **warp dust**: an ender pearl and an amethyst shard in a basin, mixed over a blaze burner, make 4. The stones need mechanical crafters.

| Item | Made on | From |
|---|---|---|
| Warp stone | Mechanical crafters | A precision mechanism, 4 warp dust, 4 amethyst shards |
| Waystone | Mechanical crafters | A warp stone, a precision mechanism, a brass casing, 2 sturdy sheets, an eye of ender, 4 of the style's stone |
| Sharestone | Mechanical crafters | A warp stone, a precision mechanism, 2 sturdy sheets, 2 dye, 3 stone bricks |
| Portstone | Mechanical crafters | A warp stone, 3 andesite casings, 2 dye, 3 stone bricks |
| Warp plate | Mechanical crafters | A dormant shard, a precision mechanism, 3 warp dust, 4 stone bricks |
| Dormant shard | Basin and press | 2 warp dust, flint |
| Scrolls | Crafting table | Warp dust, brass nuggets and paper (warp scrolls add ink) |

JEI (R on an item) shows every recipe.

## Travelling

| Use | Takes you to | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| A waystone | Any waystone you've activated | A few XP points: 1 per 100 blocks, 27 at most (27 to another dimension) |
| Warp stone (hold right-click) | Any waystone you've activated | The same XP. It lasts 128 jumps |
| Warp scroll | Any waystone you've activated | Nothing; the scroll is used up |
| Return scroll | Your nearest activated waystone | Nothing; used up |
| Bound scroll (a blank scroll used on a waystone) | That waystone, for whoever holds it | Nothing; used up |
| Portal scroll | Opens a portal to a waystone that others can step through too | Nothing; used up |
| Sharestone | Any sharestone of the same colour | Like a waystone |
| Portstone | Any waystone you've activated. Nothing can warp to a portstone | Like a waystone |
| Warp plate | The plate it's paired with (pair two with an attuned shard) | Nothing |
| Twinbound feather | A friend whose feather you linked with yours | — |
| Epitaph | Carry one when you die, and you can warp back to where you fell, once | Nothing |

Global waystones (an admin sets them) show for everyone and cost nothing to warp to.

A warp takes you and any animal on your lead, not freight. Trains and airships still carry the goods.
