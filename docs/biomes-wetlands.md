# Swamps and wetlands

Shallow water, mud and lily pads. Slimes come out at night in swamps, witches have huts here (YUNG's Better Witch Huts), frogs live in the water, and Naturalist's **alligators** wait in it. Mangrove swamps grow their trees on roots over the water.

Mangrove wood needs **Woodcutting 45** to chop. See [Skills](skills.md).
