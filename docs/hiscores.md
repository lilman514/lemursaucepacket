# Hiscores

Everyone who plays on the server is on the hiscores, ranked like RuneScape's: every skill, your total and combat levels, quest points, boss kills and collections. In game, `/hiscores` shows the tables in chat; on the web, [play.limas.ca/hiscores](https://play.limas.ca/hiscores) shows the same ones, with a page for each player.

## In game

| Command | Shows |
|---|---|
| `/hiscores` | Overall: everyone by total level, then total XP |
| `/hiscores skills` (or `bosses`, `activities`, `collections`) | Every table in that group, to click |
| `/hiscores mining` (any table's name: `elvarg`, `quest_points`, `music_discs`…) | That table |
| `/hiscores player <name>` | Someone's hiscores: their rank in every skill, their activities, boss kills and collections |

Each table shows the top ten and where you are. Click a name for that player's hiscores, a group to see its tables, or **On the website** to open the table on play.limas.ca.

## How the ranking works

- **Overall** ranks by total level, and total XP between players on the same total. **Skills** rank by XP. Everything else ranks by count.
- You're on a table once you have something on it: a skill you've trained, a boss you've killed. Overall and combat level have everyone.
- Ties go in name order.
- The server writes everyone online down every five minutes, and when you join or leave. What changed goes to the website every ten minutes, so the website can be a few minutes behind the game.
- Elvarg counts for everyone who was in the fight when she fell; a Fight Pits or Inferno win counts for the one who won it.

### Skills

Overall, each of the 16 skills (Attack, Defence, Strength, Hitpoints, Ranged, Enchanting, Cooking, Woodcutting, Fishing, Crafting, Smithing, Mining, Brewing, Agility, Farming, Construction) and combat level.

### Activities

| Table | What counts |
|---|---|
| Quest points | Quest points from the main quests (14 in all: Cook's Assistant 1, The Knight's Sword 1, Dragon Slayer I 2, Dragon Slayer II 3, The Fight Pits 2, Ashes of the Kiln 2, The Inferno 3) |
| Capes earned | Capes of every kind: skill, quest and feat capes |
| Collection log | Every kind of item you've had: held, worn, picked up or crafted |
| Quest book quests | Quests finished in the quest book |
| Raids won | Villages saved from a raid |
| Monsters slain | Creatures killed, of every kind |
| Players slain | Other players killed |
| Hours played | Time on the server |
