# Mountains, peaks and highlands

Tectonic makes the mountains here much taller and steeper than vanilla's, and Terralith adds cliffs, spires, highlands and volcanoes to them. Up high you'll find goats, emerald ore (it only generates in mountain biomes), snow and ice at the top, and views worth a waystone.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Falls are the real danger up here. Carry a water bucket, and train **Agility**: each level softens fall damage. See [Skills](skills.md).
{% endhint %}
