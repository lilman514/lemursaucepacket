# Your first week

There's no wrong way to play, but there is a quick way to get going. This is it: what most people do in their first few evenings, in roughly the order that works.

## Day one: find Lemurton

You wake up in the wild, a few minutes' walk from the capital, holding a **Compass to Lemurton**. On the way:

- Punch a tree for wood and make wooden tools. Stone tools need level 5 in the tool's skill (Mining for a pickaxe, Woodcutting for an axe), and those first levels take minutes.
- Pick up whatever you find: every vendor in Lemurton buys everything.

When you arrive:

- [ ] **Touch the Lemurton waystone** in the market square, so you can always come back. See [Waystones](waystones.md).
- [ ] **Talk to Mayor Thaddeus.** He starts *Welcome to Lemurton*, the first questline, and pays in Gold Coins. See [Lemurton](lemurton.md).
- [ ] **Open the quest book** (ESC → Quests). The first chapter, *Landfall*, is the tutorial and pays coins too.
- [ ] **Find a place to live** outside the walls: Lemurton is a safe zone where nobody can build. PvP is on everywhere else and there are no land claims, so pick your spot with care. See [The world and its rules](world.md).

{% hint style="warning" %}
When you die your things wait in a **grave** where you fell, and you drop a **Heart**: run out of hearts and you're eliminated. Read [Hearts, graves and elimination](lifesteal.md) before you take risks.
{% endhint %}

## Days two to four: levels and coins

Skills level up as you use them: mining, chopping, farming, fishing, cooking, crafting, fighting, building. The first ten levels of anything come fast. See [Skills](skills.md).

- **Iron** needs Mining 15 for the tools and a **Hardness I** tome on your pickaxe to mine the ore (the tome is crafted from andesite, copper and a book). See [Enchanting](enchanting.md).
- **Coins** come from quests, from selling to vendors (try `/worth` on anything), and from **weekly missions** (ESC → Missions, or **H**), which pay Coin Pouches. See [Coins, vendors and trading](economy.md).
- **Cook's Assistant** is the first main quest: the Cook in the butcher's shop wants milk, an egg and a pot of flour. The flour is wheat ground in a Create millstone, so it's also your first taste of Create. See [Quests](quests.md).

## Days four to seven: Create

Every stage of progress runs through Create. A first factory is a water wheel, some shafts and cogwheels, and a millstone; then a mechanical press for iron sheets, and a mixer over a blaze burner for **brass**. See [Create basics](create.md).

- **The Knight's Sword** (Mining 10, Smithing 10) is the second main quest, and it's built around Create: Brann the Smith wants a brass ingot, a precision mechanism and two iron ingots.
- **Create's machines unlock with skills**, a milestone or two a skill: mechanical drills at Mining 15, saws at Woodcutting 15, harvesters at Farming 15, mechanical crafters at Crafting 30. Anyone can use a machine a friend made. See the table in [Skills](skills.md#create-machines).

## After that

- **Dragon Slayer I** (combat level 40, Brewing 55 for the eyes of ender) takes you to the End. See [The End](end.md).
- **The Fight Pits** and **the Inferno** are the hardest fights on the server, for the Fire and Infernal capes. See [The Fight Pits and the Inferno](fight-pits.md).
- **Ore veins** never run out: find one with a vein finder and drill it with Create Ore Excavation's drilling machine (its drill heads need Mining 50).
- **Trains** (Agility 35 for stations and controls) and **airships** carry you and your goods across the world.
- Every skill at 99 earns its **cape**, and the [hiscores](hiscores.md) show how you stack up.
