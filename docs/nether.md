# The Nether

The Nether is vanilla's, filled out: Regions Unexplored adds four biomes of its own, YUNG's Better Nether Fortresses rebuilds the fortresses, and half a dozen structure mods add keeps, ports, castles, towers and camps. It's where blaze rods, nether wart, quartz and netherite come from, and Ashes of the Kiln sends you to its wither skeletons.

## Getting there

Build a portal: a frame of obsidian four wide and five tall, lit with flint and steel. Obsidian needs **Hardness III** on your pickaxe to mine, so early on take it from ruined-portal and bastion chests, or cast it in place by pouring water over lava. Portals here are linked both ways: one always leads back to the portal you came through.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Bring fire resistance if you can brew it, a stack of blocks to bridge lava, and gold armour (one piece is enough) so piglins leave you alone. Don't sleep in a bed: it explodes.
{% endhint %}

## What's there

- **Fortresses** (YUNG's Better Nether Fortresses): blazes for blaze rods, nether wart, and wither skeletons. A wither skeleton's skull only drops for a killer of **combat level 75**. See [Skills](skills.md).
- **Bastions**: piglin strongholds full of gold, and hoglins in the stables. Create: Structures Arise adds a brass bastion of its own.
- **Dungeons and Taverns**: nether keeps and ports, piglin camps, donjons and outstations, a hamlet, and skeleton towers and camps in every biome.
- **Piglin Proliferation**: piglin alchemists, who brew potions for their kin, and piglin travellers' camps.
- **Structory and Towers of the Wild**: towers and outposts with a view.

The creatures and structures that set each biome apart are on its card below; [Structures and dungeons](structures.md) and [Creatures](creatures.md) have everything.
