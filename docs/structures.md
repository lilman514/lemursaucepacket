# Structures and dungeons

The world is full of places to explore: nearly four hundred kinds of structure from twenty mods, on top of vanilla's. **Loot chests are your own** (Lootr): everyone who opens one gets their own loot, so nobody gets beaten to it.

| Mod | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **When Dungeons Arise** | Huge dungeons: fortresses, temples, ships, palaces and sky fortresses, well guarded and well stocked |
| **Dungeons and Taverns** | Taverns in every wood, firewatch towers, ruined settlements, illager camps and manors, combat shrines, and Nether and End castles |
| **Towns and Towers** | Villages and pillager outposts for nearly every biome |
| **YUNG's** | Better dungeons, mineshafts, strongholds, desert and jungle temples, ocean monuments, witch huts and Nether fortresses |
| **Create: Structures Arise** and **Let The Adventure Begin** | Create-themed ruins, airships, train stations, mines, windmills and an oil rig |
| **Structory** and **Structory: Towers** | Ruins, camps, graveyards and manors, and towers and outposts |
| **Towers of the Wild** | Tall towers in every biome, with a waystone on top |
| **Terralith** | Fortified villages, mage towers, desert outposts and underground ruins |
| **Illager Invasion** | Forts, towers, huts and a labyrinth for its new illagers |
| **Friends & Foes** | The iceologer's cabin, the illusioner's shack and training grounds, and the citadel |
| **Ice and Fire** | Dragon caves and roosts |
| **Nullscape** | A dragon's skeleton and the Rift, in the End |

{% hint style="success" %}
Structures with many variants (a village for every biome, a mineshaft for every wood) share one card that lists its kinds.
{% endhint %}
