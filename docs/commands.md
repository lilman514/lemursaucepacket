# Commands

Nearly everything here has a button as well (ESC opens the hub: see [The ESC menu](esc-menu.md)), but commands are quicker once you know them. Type them in chat: press **T**, or **/** to start with the slash.

## You and your progress

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/hiscores` | The hiscores: everyone ranked by total level. [More](hiscores.md) |
| `/hiscores skills` | Every skill's table to click (also `bosses`, `activities`, `collections`) |
| `/hiscores mining` | One table: any skill, boss, activity or collection by name |
| `/hiscores player <name>` | Someone's hiscores: every skill, boss, activity and collection |
| `/capes` | Your cape collection: what you have, how to earn the rest. [More](capes.md) |
| `/capes wear <cape>` | Put on a cape from your bag |
| `/capes off` | Take your cape off (it goes back in your bag) |
| `/hearts` | Your hearts, and where each one came from. [More](lifesteal.md) |
| `/guide` | Open the LemurSaucePacket Guide, this wiki as a book |
| `/skills` | The skills screen: every skill and your level. [More](skills.md#the-skills-screen) |
| `/skills <skill>` | That skill's guide: what every level unlocks |

## Coins and trading

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/purse` | How many Gold Coins you're carrying. [More](economy.md) |
| `/worth` | What a vendor would pay for the item in your hand |
| `/trade <player>` | Ask someone to trade (or sneak and right-click them with an empty hand). [More](economy.md) |
| `/palette buy` | Buy another Mason's Palette when you've lost yours (Construction 99, 10,000 coins). [More](skills.md) |

## Lifesteal

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/revive <player>` | Bring an eliminated player back, using the Heart in your hand. [More](lifesteal.md) |

{% hint style="info" %}
There are no teleport commands: travel is by [waystones](waystones.md), trains, airships, horses and happy ghasts. Admins have more commands (under `/lsp`), which aren't listed here.
{% endhint %}

## Handy vanilla ones

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/msg <player> <message>` | A private message (also `/tell` and `/w`) |
| `/me <action>` | Say what you're doing, in the third person |
| `/seed` | Not allowed here: the world's seed is the server's secret |
