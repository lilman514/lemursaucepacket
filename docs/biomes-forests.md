# Forests and taigas

Wood is the first thing anyone needs, and forests have the most of it: oak and birch near spawn, spruce in the taigas, and further out Terralith's sakura, lavender and moonlit groves and Regions Unexplored's redwoods, maples, willows and eucalyptus. Forests are full of life too: wolves, foxes and rabbits, and Naturalist's **bears** and **deer**, which only live in the woods.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A **dark forest** is dark enough under its canopy for monsters to spawn at noon. Light a path through it, and look out for the woodland mansions that hide there.
{% endhint %}

Some woods wait on your **Woodcutting** level before you can chop them: jungle at 15, acacia at 30, dark oak at 35, mangrove at 45, cherry at 50. See [Skills](skills.md).
