Engineering

Create is the spine

Every stage of progress runs through Create's machines. You start with a water wheel and a press, work up to brass, steam and mechanical crafters, then lay track between towns and build airships to fly over them. The rest of the pack feeds the machines or gives them a reason to run.

  • Infinite ore veins. Find them with a prospector and drill them with your own contraptions.
  • Trains and parcels. Stations, schedules, signals and a stock-keeping network.
  • Airships. Propellers, envelopes and a physics assembler, steered from a helm.
  • Hardness tiers. Iron, gold and diamonds need the right tome on your pickaxe and the Mining level to match.

The world

Lemurton, the capital

A walled city in the style of Varrock and Lumbridge stands a short walk from spawn, out of sight on purpose: your first login hands you a compass, and finding it is your first quest. Inside the walls are a market square round a great tree, a cathedral, shops on the ring streets and guards at four gatehouses.

  • Townsfolk with names and stories. More than thirty of them, each with a nametag and something to say.
  • A safe zone. Nobody can break, build or fight inside the walls, and no monsters spawn.
  • More capitals out there. Grand cities in five styles across the world, each with a name of its own.
  • A reworked world. Terralith, Regions Unexplored, When Dungeons Arise and YUNG's structures, with loot chests that are yours alone.

Progression

Skills like RuneScape

Sixteen skills, each from 1 to 99 on RuneScape's own experience curve. Attack lets you wield better weapons, Defence lets you wear better armour, Mining lets you break harder ore, and every skill unlocks something on the way: Crafting 30 for diamond gear, Brewing 55 for Eyes of Ender, Mining 15 for Create's mechanical drills. The bigger quests ask for levels before they start, a tracker shows the XP as it comes in, and the hiscores rank everyone.

Adventure

Quests worth the trip

The quest book is a road map from your first tools to the End, thirty-seven chapters of it, and every quest pays XP, coins and a roll on a reward crate. Lemurton's people have troubles of their own as well: their quests only appear in your book once someone gives them to you, RuneScape style.

  • The Ages: Landfall, First Rotation, Brass Age, Iron Roads, Skyward, Crown of Fire, Legacy.
  • Questlines for factories, farms, the railway, relics, enchanting, the bestiary and more.
  • Main quests: Cook's Assistant, The Knight's Sword, Dragon Slayer I and II, then the Fight Pits and the Inferno.
  • Weekly missions for your Create works, paid in Coin Pouches.
The quest guide

Dragon Slayer I and II

Elvarg is waiting

Dragon Slayer I takes you to the end of the world, after the Ender Dragon. Dragon Slayer II is the score Oziach never settled: the isle of Crandor, where a fire dragon in her prime sleeps on her hoard. Speak to Ned at the memorial below her hill and he takes you up to a lair of your own, so she's always there for whoever needs the fight.

  1. Slay the Ender Dragon: an Anti-dragon Shield from the mayor, three pieces of a map to the stronghold, and her island.
  2. Find Crandor: Wizard Traiborn scries it with her bottled breath. No map shows it any more.
  3. Fight alone, or open a co-op fight from Ned's window. Up to four of you, and helpers don't need the quest.
  4. If she gets you, nothing drops. Ned keeps everything you had at the memorial. No graves, no dupes.
  5. Bring her head to Oziach and earn the right to wear dragon armour.

Crandor

The memorial and the lair

A small monument at the foot of Elvarg's hill marks where the fight begins. It has a waystone, so touch it on your way up and you can come straight back.

The Kilnfolk

The Fight Pits and the Inferno

After the Ender Dragon, the Champions' Guild sends you to the Kilnfolk: ashen piglins who left the Nether for the fire mountains, and test their champions in pits of lava and blackstone. Thirty waves, alone, and Kiln-Tok-Jad at the end. Win, and the Fire Cape is yours.

  1. Earn your place at Kiln Hollow: an offering, the Ashen Trial in the Nether, and Defence, Hitpoints and Attack or Ranged at 50.
  2. Thirty waves of pit beasts: embers, cubes that split, flaming archers, brutes that heal the rest, fire Fire Resistance can't stop.
  3. Read Jad. Flames gather: raise your shield. He rises: get out of the ring. Strike a mender and they all turn on you.
  4. Fall, and lose nothing but the run. Grull drags you out with everything you had.
  5. Then the Inferno: the Wither, the Warden and Elvarg first, then Kiln-Kal-Zuk behind his drifting shield, for the Infernal Cape.

Kiln Hollow

Where the pits are

The Kilnfolk's outpost stands in the volcanic lands nearest Lemurton: the grated mouth of the Fight Pits, the sealed gate of the Inferno, the Elder's seat and Brakka's forge. The Kilnfolk Pass points the way, and its waystone brings you back.

Economy

Gold Coins, and vendors who buy anything

One currency, one stack, however many you carry. Quests and missions pay coins; every vendor in town buys whatever you bring and sells their own line. Prices come from what things are made of, so crafting never makes coins out of nothing.

  • Talk first. A vendor greets you on the first click and opens the shop on the next.
  • Buyback. Sold the wrong thing? Buy your last sales back for what you were paid.
  • Player trading. /trade swaps items and coins, with a deal timer so nobody pulls a fast one.
  • RuneScape numbers. Stacks read 2.3k, 45k, 1.2M, in yellow, white and green.

The armory

Gear, capes and relics

Create-made tools and five armour sets with full-set bonuses, every piece with a rarity, stats and a skill it needs. Capes are earned, then worn in a slot of their own or hung on an armor stand: finish a chapter, reach 99, slay the dragon. The legendary ones move.

  • Tools that save hours: a Lumber Axe that fells whole trees, an Excavator's 3x3 pickaxe, a Prospector's pickaxe that smelts what it mines, a Builder's Wand.
  • Armour sets: Prospector, Aeronaut, Brass Duelist, Compacted Diamond and Compacted Netherite.
  • Relics from the Relics mod, some of them with set synergies.
  • JEI knows where everything comes from: a "how to get" page for every item you can't craft.

The rules

Hearts on the line

PvP is on and there are no land claims. Pick your spot with care, keep your valuables close, and settle things like adults.

Lifesteal hearts

Every death costs a heart, dropped where you fell for anyone to take. Run out and you're eliminated. Kill the same player twice in half an hour and nothing drops.

Graves

Your items wait in a grave where you died, for you alone, at the price of a Grave Essence. The heart never goes in it.

Waystones, not teleports

No /tp. Activate waystones in towns, on towers and at shrines and warp between them; the rest is trains, airships, horses and happy ghasts.

Fair-play maps

A minimap and a world map for everyone, without mob radar or cave maps.

Quality of life

A launcher of its own

The launcher is the only thing you install. It signs you in with Microsoft, downloads Minecraft, Java, NeoForge and every mod, keeps them identical to the server's, and updates itself. Press Play and you land on the server.

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

The pack, the launcher and the server are free. You do need your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition.

Do I have to download every mod myself?

No. The launcher fetches all 179 of them and keeps them identical to the server's. When the pack updates, the next Play installs the update first.

How strong a PC do I need?

Anything that runs modern Minecraft well, with 8 GB of RAM free for the game (the launcher gives it 6 GB). Shaders are off by default; turn them on in the launcher's Settings if your graphics card is up to it.

Can I add my own mods?

Client-side extras that the launcher lists as optional, yes, from its Mods tab. Anything else would put you out of step with the server, which turns away mismatched mods.

What happens when I die?

You lose a heart, and it drops where you fell for anyone to pick up. Your items wait in a grave only you can open. Lose all your hearts and you're eliminated. Deaths in Elvarg's Lair are kinder: Ned keeps your things at the memorial.

Is there land claiming?

No. PvP is on and nothing is claimed, apart from Lemurton itself, where nobody can build, break or fight.

Where's the wiki?

At play.limas.ca/wiki, and in game: every player starts with the LemurSaucePacket Guide, and ESC, then Guide, opens it anywhere.

Something broke. Who do I tell?

Tell an admin in game or on voice. If the game crashed, the launcher's Home page shows the last lines of the crash and can open the crash-reports folder.

See you in Lemurton

Download the launcher, press Play, and follow the compass. The mayor has work for you.
