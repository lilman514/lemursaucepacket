# Savannas, steppes and shrublands

Dry grassland under flat-topped acacias and Regions Unexplored's huge baobabs. This is where Naturalist's big animals live: **elephants**, **giraffes**, **zebras** (which you can ride), **rhinos** and **lions** (which hunt you back). Horses, llamas and villages too.

Acacia needs **Woodcutting 30** to chop. See [Skills](skills.md).
