# Jungles and rainforests

Thick, tall and green. Jungle trees grow huge (two blocks across), with vines, cocoa pods and melons underneath, and bamboo thickets where the pandas live. Parrots and ocelots live here, and jungle temples (rebuilt by YUNG's Better Jungle Temples) hide traps and loot.

Jungle wood needs **Woodcutting 15** to chop. See [Skills](skills.md).
