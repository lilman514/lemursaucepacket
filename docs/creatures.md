# Creatures

Over a hundred creatures spawn in this world. Next to vanilla's:

- **Naturalist** adds real animals, each in the biomes it belongs in: bears and deer in the woods, elephants, giraffes, lions and zebras on the savanna, alligators in the swamps, whales and sharks at sea, and birds, snakes, butterflies and fireflies.
- **Friends & Foes** brings back Mojang's mob-vote creatures: the glare, the mauler, the moobloom, the crab, the rascal, and the iceologer and illusioner illagers.
- **Creeper Overhaul** gives most biomes a creeper of their own, each with a trick of its own when it blows.
- **Variants & Ventures** adds biome variants of zombies and skeletons.
- **Ice and Fire** adds fire, ice and lightning dragons, which live in caves and roosts rather than spawning in the open: see [Where to find things](where-to-find.md).
- **Illager Invasion** adds new illagers who live in their own forts, towers and huts (see [Structures and dungeons](structures.md)), not in the open.

{% hint style="info" %}
A creature's card lists every biome it spawns in; click one to see that biome. Monsters spawn in the dark almost anywhere, so "spawns in" for them means where they can, not where you'll always meet them.
{% endhint %}
