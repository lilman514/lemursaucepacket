# The world and its rules

## Rules

- **PvP is on** and there are **no land claims**. Pick your spot with care, keep valuables close, and settle things like adults.
- **Die and your items wait in a grave** where you fell. Only you can open it (a teammate can with your permission).
- **No waystones, no teleports.** You travel by train, airship, horse and happy ghast, so the rail network matters. Nether portals are linked properly (a portal always leads back where it came from).
- **The launcher decides the mods.** Your own extras go in its Mods tab.

## What's different from vanilla

- **Create is the spine.** Every stage of progress runs through Create machines; other mods feed it (ore veins, crops, coins) or give it a reason to exist (structures, an economy, airships).
- **Terrain:** Terralith, Regions Unexplored and Nullscape rework the Overworld and the End, with new trees and woods.
- **Structures:** When Dungeons Arise, YUNG's structures, Create-themed ruins and towns. Loot chests are per player (Lootr), so nobody gets beaten to one.
- **Ore veins:** Create Ore Excavation adds infinite veins you find with a prospector and drill with Create machines.
- **Economy:** Numismatics coins pay for quests and missions and buy from other players' vendors.
- **Skills:** RuneScape-style levels gate weapons, tools and armour and make you stronger. See [Skills](skills.md).
- **Maps:** Xaero's minimap and world map, without mob radar or cave maps (fair play).
- **Voice:** proximity voice chat. Press **V** for its settings; **O** mutes your microphone.

## Weekly missions

Brassworks Missions posts a set of Create-flavoured missions every week (ESC → Missions, or **H**). They pay coins and quest XP.
