# Snowy and frozen lands

Cold lands: snowy plains and taigas, ice spikes, frozen marshes and chasms, and Terralith's wintry forests and Siberian groves. Snow falls instead of rain, water freezes, and the animals change: white foxes, polar bears near the ice, and Naturalist's **mammoths** in the coldest places. Strays (skeletons with slowing arrows) take the place of skeletons on the surface.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Powder snow** looks like snow, but you sink into it and freeze. Leather boots let you walk on top of it, and a bucket picks it up.
{% endhint %}
