# Plains, meadows and fields

Flat and open, with good sight lines and room to build: most bases start on plains. Villages favour them, horses and donkeys graze on them, and the flower fields (vanilla's sunflower plains and meadows, Regions Unexplored's poppy, clover and barley fields, Terralith's blooming valleys) are where to farm dyes, bees and honey.

{% hint style="success" %}
Sunflowers always face east, so a field of them is a compass.
{% endhint %}
