# Farming, food and animals

Food here is Farmer's Delight: knives, a cutting board, a cooking pot, and meals that keep you fed for minutes at a time. Farming and Cooking are [skills](skills.md), so what you can plant and what you can cook grow with your levels.

{% hint style="success" %}
The quest book walks you through it. The **Homestead** chapter covers the basics (new crops, rich soil, breeding), and **Grand Kitchen** goes from the skillet to the feasts. See [Quests](quests.md#chapters).
{% endhint %}

## Why cook meals

- **Nourishment.** Farmer's Delight's soups, stews and meals give Nourishment, and so do vanilla's soups and stews here. While it lasts, your hunger bar turns gold and stops going down (healing still uses a little). It lasts from half a minute for a bowl of rice to five minutes for the best meals; the food's tooltip says how long.
- **Big meals.** A meal restores a lot of hunger and saturation at once, and meals stack to 16. Here so do mushroom stew, beetroot soup and rabbit stew, and rabbit stew fills you more than in vanilla.
- **See the numbers.** Every food's tooltip shows the hunger and saturation it gives. Hold food and your hunger bar previews what it would fill; your saturation shows as an outline over the bar (AppleSkin).
- **Drinks.** A milk bottle clears one effect. Hot cocoa (Cooking 25) clears one harmful effect. Melon juice (Cooking 15) heals a little at once.
- **Recipes need Cooking.** Most meals need a Cooking level: beetroot soup needs 5, the feasts 50. A meal past your level stays in the pot until you have it. [Skills](skills.md#cooking) lists them all.
- **Let your backpack feed you.** A Feeding upgrade (Cooking 25) in the backpack you wear eats food from the backpack when you get hungry; its filter says which foods it may use. The Advanced Feeding upgrade (Cooking 40) also lets you choose when: as soon as you're a little hungry, or only when no food would be wasted. It can feed you at once when you're hurt, too.

## The kitchen

Three things do most of the work: a knife, a cutting board and a cooking pot. In JEI, **U** over the cutting board or the cooking pot lists everything it makes.

### Knives and straw

- **Knives.** A flint knife is a flint on a stick. Golden, iron, diamond and netherite knives need a Smithing or Crafting level to make, and every knife needs an Attack level to fight with ([Skills](skills.md#gear-relics-and-blocks)).
- **Straw.** Cut grass with a knife for a chance of Straw. Harvest fully grown wheat or rice with a knife for a Straw every time. Straw makes rope, straw bales and organic compost.
- **Extra drops.** An animal killed with a knife in your hand drops something extra: leather from cows, horses and llamas, a feather from chickens, string from spiders, rabbit hide from rabbits, a shell from shulkers, and often a Ham from a pig or a hoglin.
- **Pumpkins** broken with a knife drop four pumpkin slices.

### The cutting board

Planks and sticks make one. Right-click the board with an ingredient to set it down, then right-click it again with the right tool. JEI's **Cutting Board** page lists every recipe. Fortune on the tool raises the odds of the rare results, 10 % a level.

| Tool | Cuts |
|---|---|
| Knife | Meat into cuts and mince, a cabbage into leaves, rice panicles into rice and straw, a cake into seven slices, a flower into two dyes (a crafting table gives one) |
| Axe | A log into a stripped log and Tree Bark (for organic compost) |
| Shovel | A clay block into four clay balls; gravel back again, now and then with a flint |

### The cooking pot

- **It needs heat underneath.** Stand the pot on a lit stove, a magma block or a lava cauldron, or over a lit campfire, fire or lava (it grows a tray to sit on). A blaze burner works too, and a hotter one cooks faster. Until the pot is heated, its screen says "Needs heat from below". A hopper or a Create chute between the heat and the pot still lets the heat through.
- **Up to six ingredients** go in the grid. The recipe book button shows what you can cook from what you carry.
- **Serve it.** Finished servings wait in the pot. Hover the meal to see what it's served on ("Served on: Bowl"), put bowls (or bottles) in the pot's container slot, and take the filled meals out.
- **A pot keeps its meal.** Break a pot with servings still in it and the item keeps them ("Holds 4 servings of…"). Place it again to serve the rest.
- **Hoppers and levels.** A pot that hoppers feed cooks at the Cooking level of whoever last opened it.

### Stove and skillet

- **The stove** is a campfire you can cook on (iron ingots, bricks and a campfire). Right-click raw food onto it, up to six at a time, or stand a cooking pot or a skillet on it. Light it with flint and steel. A lit stove burns whatever stands on it.
- **The skillet** (Smithing 15 to make, Attack 15 to fight with) cooks what a campfire cooks, one at a time. Hold it, hold the raw food in your other hand, stand next to a fire, campfire, lava, magma block or stove, and hold right-click. Sneak to place it as a block on a heat source, then right-click food into it. Fire Aspect on the skillet cooks faster.

### Feasts and pies

- **Feasts** (Cooking 50: roast chicken, stuffed pumpkin, honey glazed ham, shepherd's pie and more) are placed as blocks. Right-click one with a bowl to take a serving; if it needs something else, the block tells you. Break the leftovers when you're done.
- **Pies** are placed as blocks too. Right-click one to eat a slice, or use a knife on it to cut a slice to carry. A cake broken with a knife drops cake slices.

## Growing crops

- **Planting levels.** Some crops need a Farming level to plant, from beetroot, cabbages and onions at 5 to chorus flowers at 60. The full list is in [Skills](skills.md#farming).
- **Harvest when it's ripe.** Harvesting crops pays Farming XP: wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, Farmer's Delight's crops, melons and pumpkins. A crop you planted yourself pays only when it's fully grown; break it early and, like any block you placed, it pays nothing. Look at a crop and the block info at the top of your screen (Jade) shows its growth, "Mature" when it's ready.
- **Wild crops.** Farmer's Delight's crops, and some vanilla ones, grow wild. Break one for its seeds or the crop itself. JEI's information tab on each wild crop says where it grows:

| Crop | Grows wild in |
|---|---|
| Cabbages, beetroot | Beaches |
| Carrots, onions | Plains and forests |
| Potatoes | Cold lands: mountains and taigas |
| Tomatoes | Savannas and deserts |
| Rice | Ponds in swamps and jungles |

- **Buying seeds.** Saffi the Spice Merchant, at Lemurton's market, sells Farmer's Delight's seeds ([Lemurton](lemurton.md#whos-who)). Wandering traders sometimes do.
- **Rice** grows in water. Plant it in water one block deep, on dirt, with air above it, and it grows two blocks tall. Cut the rice panicles from the top on a cutting board for rice and straw. Rice needs Farming 20.
- **Tomatoes climb rope.** Hang rope above a tomato plant and the vine climbs it, bearing tomatoes all the way up. Breaking the vine leaves the rope. Two straw make four rope. Tomatoes need Farming 10.
- **Rich soil.** Organic compost (Farming 20 to make: dirt, two straw and bone meal, with rotten flesh or tree bark) turns into rich soil by itself. It turns faster in sunlight, next to water, and next to mushrooms, mycelium, podzol or more compost. Crops on rich soil get a free bone-meal boost now and then; till it with a hoe for rich farmland.
- **Mushroom colonies.** A mushroom planted on rich soil grows into a colony. Shear a grown colony for mushrooms, and it grows back.
- **Harvester's Scythe** (Farming 30, see [Gear](gear.md#tools)). Right-click a ripe crop: it harvests and replants every ripe crop in a 5 by 5 on that level, at no seed cost. It works on wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart, cabbages, onions, tomatoes and rice, and pays the same Farming XP as picking them by hand.
- **Butterflies** (Naturalist) pollinate flowers and make crops near them grow, as bees do. Catch some with a Capture Net (bamboo and string) and let them go by your fields.

## Farms that run themselves

{% hint style="info" %}
A harvester pays the Farming for each crop it reaps to everyone within 16 blocks of it, and an XP Bank keeps a share while nobody's near ([Machine XP](create-tips.md#machine-xp)). Crops you planted pay once they're fully grown.
{% endhint %}

- **Mechanical Harvester** (Farming 15 to make). On a moving contraption (a piston, bearing, gantry, minecart or train), it harvests fully grown crops and replants them, and leaves young ones alone. The harvest goes into the contraption's chests.
- **Mechanical Plough** (Farming 15). On a contraption it tills dirt into farmland and breaks loose plants and torches in its way. It throws mobs out of its way without hurting them.
- **Sprinkler** (Farming 30; the recipe makes three). Pipe a fluid into it from above. Water makes the ground below count as rained on. Liquid Fertilizer (organic compost or Tree Fertilizer mixed with water) bone-meals it. Craft a sprinkler on its own to switch between the hanging and the floor kind.
- **Rich soil by spout.** A spout pours 500 mB of Liquid Fertilizer onto organic compost and makes rich soil at once.
- **Tree farms.** A mechanical saw (Woodcutting 15) that cuts a tree's bottom log brings the whole tree down. On a moving contraption it cuts every tree it runs into. It only cuts trees that whoever placed it (or last right-clicked it) has the Woodcutting level for. Create's Tree Fertilizer grows a sapling into a tree even in a tight space.
- **Cutting by machine.** A mechanical saw, or a deployer holding a knife, does cutting-board recipes on a belt or depot, and a mechanical arm can put things on a cutting board. The **Slicer** (Cooking 25 to make) is a powered cutting board: right-click it with a knife or an axe to fit the tool, and it cuts what's on the depot or belt under it, or works over a basin. It wears the tool down, and it only works turning the right way.
- **Cooking by machine.** Cooking pots, skillets and stoves work with Create. A packager unpacks a recipe's ingredients straight into a pot, a mechanical arm puts bowls in and takes meals out, and a funnel pulls finished food. A mechanical mixer over a heated basin cooks pot recipes too, at the Cooking level of whoever last placed or right-clicked the basin. Hold **W** over a cooking pot, cutting board, stove or skillet for Ponder lessons on all of this.
- **Bread by machine.** A millstone or crushing wheels grind wheat into flour; a fan washing the flour makes dough; a fan smoking the dough (or a smoker) bakes bread.
- **Haunting.** An encased fan blowing through soul fire turns sweet berries into glow berries, and red and brown mushrooms into crimson and warped fungus.

## Animals

- **Look at an animal** and the block info (Jade) shows a baby's time left to grow up, an adult's breeding cooldown, a chicken's time to its next egg, a horse's jump height and speed, and a pet's owner. A beehive shows its bees and honey.
- **Knife kills** drop extra leather, feathers, string and ham: see [Knives and straw](#knives-and-straw).
- **Pet food.** Dog Food strengthens a tamed wolf, Horse Feed a tamed horse or other mount. The tooltip says what each one gives.
- **Hide** from boars, elephants, komodo dragons and rhinos smelts into leather.
- **A glare** (Friends & Foes, in caves) tamed with glow berries follows you and flies to dark spots where monsters could spawn, so you know where a torch is needed. It eats glow berries off cave vines too.

[Creatures](creatures.md) says where each animal lives.

## Raising a dragon

Ice and Fire's dragons can be hatched and raised. It starts with an egg, and placing one to hatch it takes Farming 70.

- **Hatching.** Place the egg where its kind wants to be: a fire egg in fire, an ice egg in water (it freezes into a Frozen Dragon Egg), a lightning egg out in the rain under open sky. After about six minutes there it hatches. Look at it to see the time left.
- **It's yours.** The hatchling comes out tame, bonded to whoever placed the egg. (JEI's note says it bonds with the closest player; it doesn't.)
- **Growing.** Feed it meat. Dragon Meal (bones and meat) makes a young dragon grow much faster; Sickly Dragon Meal stops it growing.
- **Riding.** Right-click your dragon with an empty hand. A hatchling climbs onto you; once it's big enough, you climb onto it.
- **Orders.** The Dragon Command Staff (a dragon skull on two sticks) switches your dragon between wandering, staying and escorting you. Sneak and right-click it with the staff to set or clear its home.
- **Carrying and calling.** A Dragon Horn (Crafting 45) stores your dragon and lets it out somewhere else. A Summoning Crystal (Crafting 65) bound to it brings it to you. A Dragon Bone Flute brings your flying dragons nearby down to land.
- **Scales without a fight.** Brush a grown dragon of yours for a scale or two now and then.
