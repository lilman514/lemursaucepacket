# Oceans, rivers and coasts

Water covers much of the world. Oceans hold ocean monuments (YUNG's Better Ocean Monuments), shipwrecks and ruins; warm oceans have coral reefs, and frozen ones icebergs and polar bears. Dolphins swim alongside boats, turtles nest on beaches, and Naturalist adds **whales** and **great white sharks** out at sea.

{% hint style="info" %}
Drowned sometimes carry tridents. A trident needs **Ranged 40** to use. See [Skills](skills.md).
{% endhint %}
