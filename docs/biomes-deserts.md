# Deserts, badlands and canyons

Hot and dry: it never rains here. Deserts hold cacti, dead bushes, husks (zombies that make you hungry), camels and armadillos, and desert temples rebuilt by YUNG's Better Desert Temples. The badlands' banded terracotta mesas have extra gold ore near the surface, and Terralith's canyons (Bryce Canyon's hoodoos, painted and white mesas, desert spires and oases) are some of the most striking ground in the world.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Friends & Foes' **mauler** hides under the sand with only its back showing. Watch for sand that moves.
{% endhint %}
