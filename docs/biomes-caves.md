# Caves and the deep

The underground has biomes of its own. Lush caves (moss, glow berries, axolotls and Friends & Foes' glare), dripstone caves (extra copper), and deep down the **deep dark**, where the Warden and the ancient cities are. Terralith adds caves of ice, fungus, magma and every kind of stone, and Regions Unexplored adds redstone caves, glowing bioshroom caves and the prismarite-lit Prismachasm.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The good ores need **Hardness** as well as a good pickaxe: iron needs a Hardness I tome, gold and redstone II, diamonds III. See [Enchanting](enchanting.md).
{% endhint %}
